RESEARCH SNAPSHOT · 06 OCT 2026
00 / VERDICT

You do not need a finished game. You need one complete, coherent session that makes a stranger want another run.

The evidence supports a rough-but-fun release: an estimated 10–30 minutes of repeatable content, honest “Pre-Alpha Test” labeling, and a driving loop that feels good immediately. itch.io is the first public proving ground; TapTap is the same slice after Android controls, packaging, and Chinese presentation clear their own bar.

itch.io
Release first

Smallest credible web build

The platform tolerates disclosed jank and has no review gate. The promise is a good slice, not a complete campaign.

  • 011 district + 1 car
    Committed cel-shaded Showa look; no first-impression placeholders.
  • 023–5 delivery or cab gigs
    Accept → drive → complete → payout → repeat.
  • 03A little city life
    Ambient traffic and one cartoonish cop-chase trigger.
  • 04A functional shell
    Title, pause, restart/quit, and controls on the page.
TapTap
Release after mobile pass

Same slice, stricter delivery

Use TapTap International’s formal Testing status. Incompleteness is allowed; review, packaging, touch UX, and store assets are not optional.

  • 01Real-phone touch controls
    Steer, throttle, and brake must work for a first-time player.
  • 02Stable Android performance
    Target 60 fps on a mid-range phone; verify the actual device.
  • 03Chinese + English
    Store text, screenshots, and at least the visible UI strings.
  • 04Review-safe APK
    Custom package, versionCode ≥ 1, own keystore, clean asset proof.

02 / SAFE TO CUT

What may stay rough

Keep scope disciplined. The first release is an experiment in player behavior and publishing, not the final content promise.

Content volumeOne compact district, one car, and a handful of gigs are enough.
TutorialListed controls can carry v1; add a mini-tutorial in the first update.
Audio depthA few licensed SFX and one or two music tracks are acceptable.
Persistent progressionSession-only progress is acceptable when clearly disclosed as a test.
Options & peripheralsDeep graphics options, localization beyond essentials, and web gamepad support can wait.
Big systemsMultiplayer, weather, day/night, and sophisticated pedestrians can wait.

04 / PLATFORM TRAPS

Fail here before the players arrive

itch.io web export

  • ≤ 1,000 files and ≤ 500 MB extracted total.
  • ≤ 200 MB for any single file—including Godot’s index.pck.
  • Compress textures/audio before upload; the total limit can pass while the PCK limit fails.
  • Use “In development,” publish instantly, and keep a devlog cadence.

TapTap APK & review

  • Custom package name; versionCode cannot be 0.
  • Sign with your own keystore—never a debug/public certificate.
  • Gameplay-matching screenshots, same dimensions, no device UI; promo art needs the logo.
  • Keep proof for original or purchased assets. No ripped anime IP, gore, gambling, sexual, or political content.

TapTap Testing path

  • The APK must pass review before the status can become “Testing.”
  • Testing may be unlimited, capped, or activation-code based.
  • TapTap asks for a test plan and data-wipe/IAP policy; editors may assign test visibility.
  • Verified-developer review is documented as within 2 business days; game updates within 24 hours.

Mainland-China caveat

  • Use TapTap International for this free no-IAP test.
  • Current TapTap CN treatment of no-IAP test builds without an ISBN could not be fully verified.
  • One developer publicly paused a CN test pending ISBN; do not generalize more than that precedent proves.
  • Mainland reach and monetization require a separate publishing/compliance plan later.
05 / RELEASE SEQUENCE

Learn in the cheapest order

The point is not just downloads. It is learning whether strangers finish a gig, retry, comment, and return.

Clear the nine ship gates

Protect the core session. Cut features rather than widening the bug surface.

Publish the web Pre-Alpha on itch.io

State what is included, missing, and next; list controls and make comments easy to use.

Turn feedback into visible updates

Reply, post devlogs, and prioritize friction revealed by actual play—not speculative feature volume.

Do the Android + Chinese pass

Real-phone controls, performance, signing, package identity, store assets, and visible localization.

Enter TapTap International Testing

Use the sanctioned unfinished-game lane, then earn distribution through comments and retention.
