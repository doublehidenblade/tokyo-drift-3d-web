**Answer (model summary of the grounded search).**

### **1. 1970s Japanese City Route Buses (Long-Wheelbase & Length Variants)**

* **Standard Envelope (All Models):** Under Japanese vehicle regulations of the 1970s, overall width was standardized at **2,490 mm**, while overall height ranged between **3,050 mm and 3,150 mm** (depending on bodywork and roof equipment).
* **Mitsubishi Fuso MR470 / MR480 / B805 (1960s–1976):**
  * *MR470:* Standard wheelbase **4,800 mm**; overall length ~**10,250 mm**.
  * *MR480:* Shorter wheelbase **4,370 mm** (~**9,750 mm** length).
  * *MR490 / MR420 / B805L:* Longer/maximum wheelbase variants existed at **5,250 mm** and **5,650 mm**, yielding overall lengths of **10,750 mm** and **11,150–11,250 mm**.
* **Mitsubishi Fuso MP Series (MP117 / MP517, introduced 1976):**
  * *MP117M / MP517M:* Wheelbase **5,300 mm**; overall length ~**10,750 mm** (~10.75 m).
  * *MP117N / MP517N (Long Wheelbase):* Wheelbase **5,800 mm**; overall length ~**11,400 mm** (~11.4 m).
* **Isuzu BU Series (BU05 / BU10 / BU15 / BU20, 1965–1980):**
  * *BU05:* Wheelbase **4,800 mm**; overall length ~**10,000–10,200 mm**.
  * *BU10:* Wheelbase **5,000 mm**; overall length ~**10,400 mm**.
  * *BU15:* Wheelbase **5,200 mm**; overall length ~**10,650 mm**.
  * *BU20 (Long Wheelbase):* Wheelbase **5,500 mm**; overall length ~**11,100 mm** (~11.1 m).
* **Hino RE / RC Series (RE100 / RE120 / RE140 & RC300 / RC320, 1967–1982):**
  * *RE100 / RC300:* Wheelbase **4,800 mm**; overall length ~**10,050 mm**.
  * *RE120 / RC320:* Wheelbase **5,200 mm**; overall length ~**10,500–10,600 mm**.
  * *RE140 / RC320 (Long Wheelbase variant):* Wheelbase **5,670 mm**; overall length ~**11,250–11,400 mm**.
* **Nissan Diesel (UD) 4R / U Series (4R92/94/104/105 & U20/U30, 1960s–1980):**
  * *4R92 / 4R94:* Wheelbase **4,800 mm** and **5,000 mm**; overall lengths ~**10,000–10,350 mm**.
  * *4R104 / 4R105 / 4R110:* Wheelbase **5,400 mm** and **5,650 mm**; overall lengths ~**10,800 mm** and **11,200 mm**.
  * *U20L / U30L (Mid-length):* Wheelbase **5,200 mm**; overall length ~**10,650 mm**.
  * *U20N / U30N (Long Wheelbase):* Wheelbase **5,600 mm**; overall length ~**11,250 mm**.

### **2. Daihatsu Midget MP4 & MP5 Three-Wheel Trucks (1960–1972)**

* **Daihatsu Midget MP4 (1960–1962):**
  * *Dimensions (L x W x H):* **2,885 mm × 1,296 mm × 1,510 mm** (cab height to canvas/steel roof apex).
  * *Wheelbase:* **1,810 mm**.
* **Daihatsu Midget MP5 (1962–1972):**
  * *Dimensions (L x W x H):* **2,970 mm × 1,295 mm × 1,455 mm** (all-steel cab roof).
  * *Wheelbase:* **1,905 mm**.
* **Longer-Bed / Extended Variants:**
  * The MP4 (1960) was itself the extended-bed redesign, lengthening the original MP2/MP3 bed by 200 mm.
  * The MP5 (1962) further extended overall length and bed volume by another 85 mm (bed length: 1,260 mm vs. 1,175 mm on MP4).
  * *Panel Van Variant (MP5V):* Length **2,990 mm**, width **1,295 mm**, height **1,465 mm**, wheelbase **1,905 mm**.
* **High-Roof Variant:** **Unknown** (no factory high-roof cab variant was published or offered for the MP4/MP5 truck series).
