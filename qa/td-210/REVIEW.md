The [visual record](review/visual-review.json) lists all **78 personally opened final sheets** with independently recomputed SHA256, all 345 reviewed lot records, and 21 separately opened originals/references. This is not merely a count of files present. The 50 exceptions comprise 34 internal/back lots behind original neighboring envelopes, 14 Ring/bridge obstructions, and two concave-wing camera positions (1151 and 1331). I read each reason and checked its paired front/overhead sheet; original views for 1046, 1073, 1151 and 1331 corroborate the visibility limits. Unannotated overheads can be almost featureless roof planes; cyan outlines identify the target but do not establish a finished facade. Acceptance of those exceptions also depends on the unchanged lot geometry, measured module composition and independently rerun expanded/eave checks.

The street result is credible modular Showa market architecture at the requested bounded scope: signed recessed fronts, covered trading counters, corrugated/tiled service elevations and small loading props replace the former blank shells. Main streets still expose broad, plain service sides, and repeated hall/sign rhythms are noticeable. The golden fog limits depth, and the result is less richly dressed than the illustrative mock. Those are honest visual limits, not a claim of matching every decorative element of the illustration. Source inspection shows clipping/repetition at measured size, identity-scale placement and unchanged original footprints, rather than a stretched whole-building GLB.

The native rerun expands **923,405 market-kit triangles**, with 523 mesh resources, 18 materials and 55,045,424 serialized geometry-array bytes. Roof caps also participate in the original world batch; per-lot budgets include them. Maximum complete lot is 3,926 triangles against 15,000. The eave check measures actual upward non-ink triangles at low-eave height on every frontage; removing the relevant triangles for lot1022 produces 0m² against a 43.2m² requirement and is caught. Shared vertex checks use real native transforms; the dummy renderer is unsuitable for that claim. Unique cropped placements retain their recorded clipping bounds. Bay rays check the intended void above solid counters, not universal pedestrian accessibility.

The repeated control’s equal signature covers quantized expanded positions/normals/UVs/colors and material names; it is not a proof of every possible material property. Source material inspection, shared texture hashes and the actual AnimeLook pass supply the additional contract evidence. Final sharing duplicates a mesh per spatial cell to preserve material conversion behavior; the earlier 0.162MB global-sharing number is superseded by **0.324MB**. Prototype retained static allocation slightly increases (2,448,396→2,518,346 bytes), so the 12× array reduction must not be described as a 12× memory reduction.
