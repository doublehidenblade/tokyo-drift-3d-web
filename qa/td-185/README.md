# td-185 evidence — Lower City first pass (city plan, road system, signs, minimal gig loop)

Every image here was produced by the tools in this PR from the real `scenes/lower_city/lower_city.tscn`
(Godot 4.7.2, gl_compatibility / opengl3, 1280×720 unless noted, chase camera behind the car) and was
opened and checked before being listed. Mirrored to tokyo-drift-3d-web `qa/td-185/` (commit-pinned raw URLs
in the PR body).

## Triptychs — [REFERENCE] art-book mock · [IMPLEMENTATION] art-book mock · in-game

Mocks: tokyo-drift-3d-web `art-book/lower-city/*.png` at commit `c50959b` (the web publisher's run deleted
`art-book/` from web `main`). **The real-photo panel is missing:** this cloud container's network policy
denies `commons.wikimedia.org`, `upload.wikimedia.org`, `api.openverse.org` and `www.flickr.com`, so no
real reference photos could be fetched. Fix: allow those domains in the environment's Network access
(Custom → Allowed domains); a follow-up then adds the photo panels.

- `triptych/kotobuki.jpg` — 寿 repair row under the Ring. In-game: the trench floor under the Ring's lid
  (dark concrete sky, dark repair-bay mouths along the walls, an arc-welder flare stuttering in a bay).
  Gap vs the mocks: no Hakosuka on stands, no mechanics, no bay interiors yet — the greybox carries the
  space and the light, not the props.
- `triptych/kamome.jpg` — 鴎 market lanes. In-game: corrugated eaves with fascia boards and paper lanterns
  on both sides. Gap: no 鮮魚 / 水産 blade signs, fish crates, kei vans or workers yet.
- `triptych/chidori.jpg` — 千鳥 authored night. In-game: the warm red-amber night, a slalom of lit blade
  signs lettered in-scene (小料理さくら, 食堂まつ, おでん, ストリップ劇場 …). Closest of the five to its mock.
  Gap: no lanterns strung across, no booth / sealed-glass storefronts.
- `triptych/tenjin.jpg` — 天神 gateway. In-game: the district gateway arch with 天神 TENJIN over Ginko-dori
  between the slab walls. Gap: the mock's truss gantry and checkpoint booths are not built.
- `triptych/shinkai.jpg` — 深海 (reference mock only; no implementation mock exists). In-game: a pipe gantry
  over the haul road at 3.8 m with pipe runs between gantries, the sulfur-yellow haze. Gap: the spherical

- `triptych/daikoku.jpg`, `triptych/depots-ring.jpg` — no art-book mock exists for Daikoku, the depots or
  the Ring: in-game pairs only (quay cranes and sodium flood masts with painted pools; container stacks;

## Tests (logs in `tests/`)

| suite | result |
|---|---|
| `tools/test_lower_city_build.gd` | builds in ~1.2 s; probes on the trench floor, the Ring lid and deck, a bridge crown, a dip; Tenjin Odori clear under the Ring's ramps; dressing and atmosphere present; every plan sign built, every glyph present, **no sign post in a carriageway** |
| `tools/test_lower_city_drive.gd` | CityDriver tour of 21 legs through every district, the Ring, ramps, trench floor, alleys, a bridge: 19.6 km, **0 stuck, 0 recoveries** |
| `tools/test_lower_city_signs.gd` | a first-timer reading only the built signs: **3837/3837** road→district with the paper map's knowledge, 3715/3837 on signs alone; 12 sign-decided cross-city trips driven **12/12**, 0 stuck, 0 recoveries |
| `tools/test_lower_city_gig.gd` | 4 gigs end to end: **4/4 on time**, cargo 100 / 100 / 100 / 95 % (run 3 clipped the corner of a Kamome market alley pulling out of the 競り窓口 bay at 12 km/h: one 3 % knock), 49–56% of the limit used, ¥16,120; a deliberate wall crash wrecks the cargo |

The four logs come from one run of all four suites in parallel. `drive.log` and `gig.log` each carry one
`WARNING: Jolt Physics job system exceeded the maximum number of jobs` at load: that only happens with four
engines sharing this container's cores. `gig-solo.log`, the gig suite run alone, has no warning and gives
the same numbers.

Plan gates (`build_city.py`, the build fails otherwise): no traps; ≥ 3 meaningfully different routes for
every ordered district pair; sign-following first-timer 3837/3837; every gig pair reachable; every ramp
over-crossing ≥ 3.8 m headroom; no Ring pier in a carriageway.

## Performance (1280×720 chase frames, `CITY_STATS` lines from the capture)

107–154 draw calls and 42k–72k primitives per frame across the stations above; about 60 of those draw
calls are the car (52 of them its wheels: the shared car model draws each wheel as ~13 primitive meshes —
a cross-map follow-up, not changed here). The city itself: ~45–95.
